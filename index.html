Good to know

A few straight answers.

Do I need an account?

No. The whiteboard opens directly in your browser.

Where does my work go?

Your notes are saved in this browser's local storage. There is no account or cloud sync; another browser will not have the same board.

Can I restore a JSON export?

The current version exports JSON but does not import it. Keep a separate copy of important work, and use PNG when you need to share an image.

Can I work with others live?

Live collaboration is not available yet. You can export a PNG to share a visual snapshot.
